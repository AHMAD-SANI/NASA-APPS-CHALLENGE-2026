Global boundary support

The resolver dynamically indexes and downloads geoBoundaries gbOpen datasets for ADM0-ADM5 when those datasets exist for a country. Downloaded files are cached locally under GEOBOUNDARIES_CACHE_DIR or fires/data/boundaries/cache by default. Country coverage and available administrative levels are determined by the live/versioned geoBoundaries release index. Source attribution and licence metadata are included in resolved region responses.
